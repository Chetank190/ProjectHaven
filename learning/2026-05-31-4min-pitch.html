04The script

1 · Team intro

Hi — we're the team behind Haven Matrix. I'm [Name — Role], with me are [Name — Role] and [Name — Role].

We came to Spark Hack with one question: what's the most human problem we could put a GPU behind? Not another chatbot — something that helps a real person on a cold night in Toronto find a safe place to sleep.

2 · Elevator pitch
0:30–1:00

What: a dual-gateway triage that routes unhoused people — and the caseworkers helping them — to the right Toronto services, in real time.

How: you speak; a local model compiles your needs into JSON; cuML runs a constraint-aware nearest-neighbour search on 128 GB — a ranked route in under a second.

Why: shelters run ~97% full; Central Intake fields ~4,000 calls a day. People in crisis can't navigate hundreds of fragmented services. We do it for them.

3 · It in action
1:00–2:00

Gateway A — caseworkers. They speak a client's situation — "man, mid-forties, no ID, needs a bed and a hot meal tonight." In under a second: a ranked itinerary — nearest shelter with beds free, walk times, live occupancy, a transit badge — plus a warm-handoff phone script they read straight to the shelter.

Gateway B — a public kiosk, voice only. Tap a glowing orb, speak. It asks up to three eligibility questions out loud, you tap to confirm each, and it talks back a walking route and a reservation code. No keyboard. No ID. No app.

liveA GPU-vs-CPU benchmark on every request · a shelter-capacity ticker · weather-aware routing that folds in warming centres on a cold alert.

best partA crisis gate fires before the AI ever runs — self-harm → 988; a medical emergency → 911 with the nearest ER on screen. And all of it keeps working offline.

4 · How we built it
2:00–3:30

Stack: React + Vite + TypeScript front end; FastAPI back end; RAPIDS cuDF/cuML on GPU; Nemotron-30B via llama.cpp with a Gemma NIM and NVIDIA cloud behind it; Parakeet ASR; NeMo Guardrails; SQLite for cases, auth and reservations.

Principle: the LLM is a deterministic compiler, not a decision-maker. Every model call has a strict Pydantic schema and a regex fallback; every GPU call has a pandas/scikit-learn fallback. Four-tier model chain, CKAN-to-cache data chain — it never hard-fails.

Flow: PII scrub → injection check → guardrails → crisis gate → LLM compile → constraint masking (ID, sobriety, gender, group) → cuML solve + composite scoring → TTC transit join.

The bet: 128 GB unified memory holds all seven matrices and the model in one address space — no PCIe serialization. The CPU path runs in parallel, so the on-screen benchmark is a live measurement. Hardest parts: prompt injection (blocked before the model), privacy (one scrub chokepoint, no audio off-device), and a voice UI that never cuts vulnerable people off mid-sentence.

5 · Closing
3:30–4:00

Who's it for? City caseworkers and street outreach teams. The 211 network. Shelter operators — live capacity plus an anonymized shadow census of demand. Public health. And, through the kiosk, unhoused people themselves, with no barrier to entry.
